Cryptography

  • Identity · long-term X25519 + ECDSA-P256 signing key per device
  • Session · X3DH-lite handshake → 32-byte conversation root key
  • Per-message · HKDF-SHA256 derived AES-256-GCM key, fresh nonce, AAD-bound counter
  • Forward secrecy · ephemeral DH ratchet on every outgoing message
  • Verification · 60-digit safety numbers — compare in person or over a trusted channel
  • Identity binding · Microsoft account (Entra ID / personal MSA) with Microsoft Authenticator MFA

          How to get started

          01

          Get a free Microsoft account

          If you don’t already have one, sign up at signup.live.com. It costs nothing, you can use any email address (it doesn’t need to be @outlook.com), and it gives you the verified identity Platytalk uses to bind your keys.

          02

          Secure your account

          Open account.microsoft.com/security and turn on:

          • Two-step verification — mandatory for every Platytalk login.
          • Microsoft Authenticator on your phone (download) and approve sign-ins with a tap or face/fingerprint.
          • Passkey or Windows Hello for password-less sign-in.
          • Recovery info (alternate email + phone) so you can never be locked out.
          • Remove old app passwords and review recent activity.
          03

          Sign in to Platytalk

          Click 🔑 Sign in with Microsoft above (or the same button in the desktop app). You’ll be redirected to Microsoft, asked to approve the Authenticator prompt, then sent back. Platytalk receives your verified identity — not your password.

          04

          Generate your keys

          On first sign-in, your device generates an X25519 + ECDSA keypair locally. The public halves are uploaded so others can encrypt to you. The private halves stay in your browser’s IndexedDB (or in %APPDATA%\PlatypusTools\Platytalk on desktop) and are never seen by the relay.

          05

          Add a contact

          Share your handle (shown next to your name, e.g. platy-7K9F2) with someone, or paste theirs into the add by handle box. You can also generate an invite QR from the desktop app.

          06

          Verify safety numbers

          Open the contact, tap Verify, and compare the 60-digit safety number with them in person, on a video call, or via another secure channel. If both match, you have proof there’s no man-in-the-middle.

          07

          Set disappearing & multi-device

          Per-conversation timer makes messages self-destruct on every device. Sign in on another browser or the desktop app with the same Microsoft account to sync. Deleting a message everywhere tombstones it on every linked device of every participant.

          Why Microsoft sign-in?

          Platytalk doesn’t want to manage your password, can’t SIM-swap your phone, and never sees your MFA codes. By delegating identity to Microsoft you get enterprise-grade account protection (Authenticator, passkeys, conditional access if your org uses Entra ID) for free — and Platytalk only ever receives a signed assertion that you are who you say.
